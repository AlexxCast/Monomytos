# Percorso del Velo e ritratti dei personaggi

Le due vignette del filone Villain usano lo stesso stile delle altre. I ritratti (n_*) sono i volti dei personaggi che parlano nei dialoghi: formato 3:4, solo testa e spalle.

## s_fonderia.png (Storia: Missione: la Fonderia in fiamme)

Problema: il cielo in alto è un riquadro separato, la scena deve essere unica.

Vertical 9:16 portrait illustration (taller than wide, e.g. 768x1344) for a full-screen animated comic scene on a phone. Composition rule: the key subject and action sit between 12% and 58% of the image height; the bottom 40% holds only ground, smoke, crowd shadows or dark background, because speech bubbles cover it; leave a little extra background on every side because the image will slowly zoom and pan. Bold cartoon comic-book illustration, thick black ink outlines, cel shading, halftone dots, night city under a cosmic sky, high contrast, saturated colors. Palette: teal and cyan for heroes and the night sky, purple and red for the Veil, the corrupted and the evil star. Characters are shown as anonymous silhouettes, seen from behind or in shadow, or as distant figures: no recognizable characters, no superheroes from movies or comics, no logos. The image must contain no writing of any kind: no text, letters, numbers, captions, speech bubbles, sound effect words, signature or watermark. One single full-bleed illustration: no comic panels, no inset boxes, no borders. Scene: inside a huge burning steel foundry: molten metal pouring from crucibles, giant presses, armed workers with purple eyes charging through the flames, trapped workers behind a fallen beam.
