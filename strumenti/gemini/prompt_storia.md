## s_metropoli.png (Storia: Prologo: il mondo in equilibrio)

Problema: era una delle prime 8 vignette, in uno stile diverso dalle altre.

Vertical 9:16 portrait illustration (taller than wide, e.g. 768x1344) for a full-screen animated comic scene on a phone. Composition rule: the key subject and action sit between 12% and 58% of the image height; the bottom 40% holds only ground, smoke, crowd shadows or dark background, because speech bubbles cover it; leave a little extra background on every side because the image will slowly zoom and pan. Bold cartoon comic-book illustration, thick black ink outlines, cel shading, halftone dots, night city under a cosmic sky, high contrast, saturated colors. Palette: teal and cyan for heroes and the night sky, purple and red for the Veil, the corrupted and the evil star. Characters are shown as anonymous silhouettes, seen from behind or in shadow, or as distant figures: no recognizable characters, no superheroes from movies or comics, no logos. The image must contain no writing of any kind: no text, letters, numbers, captions, speech bubbles, sound effect words, signature or watermark. One single full-bleed illustration: no comic panels, no inset boxes, no borders. Scene: a huge modern metropolis at night seen from a high rooftop, warm city lights everywhere, three small anonymous hero silhouettes standing on different rooftops watching over the streets, a calm blue-green starry sky with soft nebulae.
