# Minacce rifatte con il soggetto più in alto (Alessio 7/11: coperto dal riquadro dell effetto)

## c_affondocalcolato.png (Carte creazione: Affondo Calcolato)

Problema: ad Alessio non piaceva (spada che trafigge una foglia).

Vertical 2:3 portrait illustration (taller than wide, e.g. 848x1264), one single continuous scene that fills the entire image edge to edge. Medium-wide shot: the main subject is drawn fairly small and compact, fully visible, centered horizontally and placed a little ABOVE the middle of the image (its center at about 40% from the top), with open sky or background above and around it that continues uninterrupted behind the subject. The lower third of the image is only ground, smoke or shadows, with nothing important in it. Nothing important near the top edge. Background and subject are part of the same space, with no lines, frames or boxes separating them. Same art style as the Monomythos story scenes: bold cartoon comic-book illustration, thick black ink outlines, cel shading, halftone dots, night city under a swirling teal and purple cosmic nebula sky, high contrast, saturated colors, atmospheric glow. This is a generic character-creation card that any hero can use: it must NOT show or suggest any specific character. No faces, no recognizable characters, no superheroes from movies or comics, no logos, no emblems, no symbols of real organizations. Only objects, places, anonymous hands or anonymous silhouettes in shadow. No writing of any kind: no text, letters, numbers, captions, speech bubbles, signature or watermark. Not a comic page: no panels, no inset rectangle, no border, no frame. Mood: raw energy being released, bright magenta and cyan glow. Scene: an anonymous fencer in dark silhouette, seen from the side, frozen in a long precise lunge, the thin blade of light extended straight ahead and hitting exactly the glowing center of a floating ring of magenta and cyan energy, faint geometric guide lines and angle arcs drawn in light around the blade show the calculated trajectory.
